Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence (KMTC) is a faith-driven annual gathering for media professionals, technologists, creatives, innovators, and Kingdom builders. It exists at the intersection of faith, media, and technology — equipping people who believe their skill is a calling. If you create, build, or innovate for God's glory, KMTC is for you.
KMTC is always seeking Kingdom-minded professionals who carry excellence, integrity, and a heart for God's purposes in media and technology. Board membership is by invitation and discernment. To express interest, reach out via hello@kmtc.com with your background, area of expertise, and how you believe you can contribute to the KMTC vision.
