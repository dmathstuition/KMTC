Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence · Nigeria

Our commitment to you: KMTC will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your information is used only to facilitate your participation in KMTC events and to keep you informed about our community.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence (KMTC) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you register for, attend, or interact with KMTC events, websites, and communications. We are committed to handling your data responsibly, transparently, and in alignment with our Kingdom values.

Section 01

Who We Are

Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence (KMTC) is a faith-driven movement and annual conference organisation based in Nigeria. We operate the KMTC event, website, registration platform, and associated community channels.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, KMTC is the data controller responsible for your personal information. You can reach us at hello@kmtc.com for any privacy-related enquiries.

Section 02

Data We Collect

We collect only the personal information necessary to facilitate your participation in KMTC and to improve our services. The following table outlines what we collect and why:

Data Type Examples Purpose
Identity Data First name, last name Registration & event check-in
Contact Data Email address, phone number Confirmation emails, event communications
Professional Data Role, organisation, industry Track selection, community matching
Location Data City, country Event logistics, community insights
Preference Data T-shirt size, group attendance Event management & logistics
Engagement Data How you heard about KMTC, expectations Programme improvement & outreach
Technical Data IP address, browser type (website only) Website security & analytics

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, government ID numbers, or health data. KMTC 2026 is a free event and requires no payment information.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used solely for legitimate purposes connected to your participation in KMTC. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Process and confirm your event registration and issue your unique registration code.
  • Send you event-related communications including confirmation emails, reminders, schedule updates, and speaker announcements.
  • Manage event logistics including check-in, track allocation, and resource distribution.
  • Invite you to join the KMTC WhatsApp community and other official channels.
  • Improve future editions of KMTC through feedback, attendance data, and engagement analytics.
  • Send you updates about future KMTC events and initiatives (you may opt out at any time).
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We will never use your data for purposes unrelated to KMTC without your explicit consent.

Section 04

Data Sharing

KMTC does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party platforms that help us operate the event (e.g. Google for registration data storage, email service providers). These partners are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Event Partners & Sponsors: Only aggregated, non-identifiable statistics (e.g. "500 attendees") are shared with sponsors. We do not share your personal details with sponsors without your explicit consent.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose your information to comply with applicable legal obligations.
Section 05

Storage & Security

Your personal data is stored securely in Google Sheets and associated Google Workspace services, which are protected by Google's industry-leading security infrastructure. Access to your data is restricted to authorised KMTC team members only.

  • We retain your data for a maximum of two years following the event, after which it is securely deleted unless you have consented to ongoing communications.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at hello@kmtc.com.
  • While we implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, no system is entirely impenetrable. If you become aware of any security issue, please notify us immediately.
Section 06

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and gather anonymised analytics data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our site.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it. These are anonymised and non-identifying.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some website features.

Section 07

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data held by KMTC. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@kmtc.com:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your data for marketing purposes at any time.

Right to Portability

Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for communications at any time by clicking unsubscribe.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

KMTC events and digital platforms are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without consent, please contact us immediately at hello@kmtc.com and we will promptly delete such information.

Section 09

Third-Party Links

Our website and communications may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, and external services. These are provided for your convenience and do not constitute an endorsement.

KMTC is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external platforms you visit.

Section 10

Policy Changes

KMTC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or organisational structure. The most current version will always be published on our website with the date of the latest update.

Significant changes will be communicated to registered attendees via email. Continued engagement with KMTC following policy updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
