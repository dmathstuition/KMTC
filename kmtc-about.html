About

Kingdom Media & Tech
Confluence

Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence (KMTC) is a Kingdom-driven movement and annual flagship gathering that exists to align faith, media, and technology for cultural and spiritual impact.

KMTC exists to close the existing gap between media, creatives, and Christian faith — equipping, connecting, and commissioning Kingdom professionals to influence culture without compromising conviction.
