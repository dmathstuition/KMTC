About KMTC

Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence

Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence (KMTC) is a faith-driven movement and annual conference that brings together media professionals, technologists, creatives, innovators, church leaders, and Kingdom builders to align skill, innovation, and influence with God's purpose.

KMTC is not just an event. It is: A movement — A platform — An ecosystem for Kingdom-minded creators, technologists, and innovators. It exists at the intersection of Faith, Media, Technology, Innovation, and Culture.

A Movement A Platform An Ecosystem Faith · Media · Tech
Why KMTC Exists

Many Kingdom-minded creatives work in isolation without mentorship or structure.

Most struggle to integrate faith with excellence in the workplace.

Kingdom voices are underrepresented in global tech and media conversations.

The church and the tech/media world operate in silos, limiting Kingdom influence.

Faith × Technology

Bridging the sacred and the digital to create Kingdom-aligned innovation and lasting cultural impact.

Creativity × Calling

Empowering creatives to pursue excellence in craft as an act of worship unto God.

Skill × Spiritual Alignment

Developing professionals who are technically excellent and spiritually grounded.

Mission & Vision

Mission
Equip · Connect · Commission

To equip, connect, and commission believers in media and technology through gatherings, training, collaboration, and strategic partnerships.

  • Equip Kingdom creatives with practical skills and spiritual clarity
  • Create a collaborative ecosystem for faith-driven innovation
  • Bridge the gap between church, media, and technology
  • Amplify Kingdom voices through media, platforms, and partnerships
  • Build sustainable structures for long-term impact
Vision
Raise · Deploy · Impact

To raise a generation of Spirit-led media and technology leaders who use their gifts to advance God's Kingdom, transform culture, and solve real-world problems with excellence.

  • A generation of Kingdom-minded media and tech leaders
  • Leaders who shape culture and systems for God's glory
  • A global community deployed for Kingdom impact
  • Excellence in every creative and technological endeavour

Core Values

What Drives Us

Seven values that define how KMTC builds, creates, and moves together as a Kingdom community.

Christ-Centeredness Excellence Integrity Innovation Collaboration Stewardship Impact
01
Christ-Centeredness

Everything we build, create, and connect begins and ends with Christ — our anchor and our purpose.

02
Excellence

God deserves excellence in media and technology — not mediocrity. We pursue the highest standard.

03
Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and accountability in every relationship, partnership, and creative output.

04
Innovation

Spirit-led innovation that solves real-world problems and advances the Kingdom through creative thinking.

05
Collaboration

Kingdom impact multiplies when we co-labor in community. We build together, not in isolation.

06
Stewardship

We faithfully manage our gifts, resources, platforms, and influence as God's trusted stewards.

07
Impact

We measure success by lasting transformation in culture and society — not by numbers or noise.
