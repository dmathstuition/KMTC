What is KMTC?

The Kingdom Media & Tech Confluence is a faith-driven movement and annual conference that unites media professionals, technologists, creatives, innovators, church leaders, and Kingdom builders to align their skills, innovation, and influence with God's purpose.

We exist at the powerful intersection of Faith, Media, Technology, Innovation, and Culture — creating a convergence, movement, and platform for lasting Kingdom impact.

Why KMTC Exists

The world is shaped by media narratives, digital platforms, and technology innovations. But Kingdom-minded creatives and technologists face unique challenges.

Isolation

Many work alone without a supportive community that understands both faith and excellence.

Lack of Structure

Without mentorship and systematic guidance, talented individuals struggle to reach their potential.

Integration Gap

Difficulty balancing faith convictions with professional excellence and industry standards.

Underrepresentation

Kingdom voices are often marginalized in media and technology spaces that shape culture.

KMTC closes this gap by equipping, connecting, and commissioning professionals to influence culture without compromising conviction.
